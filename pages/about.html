My Philosophy

I Solve Business Problems with Engineering.

I'm Syed Danish Raza, a Web Solutions Developer working worldwide. I specialize in building custom, high-speed websites, custom-tailored CRMs, and backend automations for local service companies. I partner directly with business owners who are tired of bloated agency retainer fees, slow websites, and leads slipping through the cracks.

Our Mission

To help local service businesses compete and win online. I do this by replacing slow templates and complex SaaS tools with premium, high-speed, custom-coded web architectures that capture and qualify leads on autopilot.

Our Vision

To set a new standard for local business web design. A standard where websites aren't treated as static brochures, but as primary business assets that drive bookings, eliminate admin work, and build immediate local trust.

Operating Values

The Principles That Guide Every Build

Three non-negotiable standards I apply to every project — from first call to post-launch support.

01

Clear Communication

No hidden jargon, no automated tickets, and no disappearing acts. You work directly with me. I provide clear timelines, weekly reports, and realistic outcomes from start to finish.

02

Clean Hand-Crafted Code

I do not build with heavy drag-and-drop page builders. Everything is custom-coded in semantic HTML5, pure CSS3, and modern JavaScript, ensuring fast loads and secure setups.

03

A website is successful only if it brings you customers. I structure layouts around CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) to guide visitors to your contact form or phone line.

How I Work

My Structured Design & Dev Process

A transparent, step-by-step roadmap that keeps your project moving efficiently from kick-off to launch.

1

Discovery & Competitive Audit

We audit your existing analytics, outline your core client persona (e.g., dental patients, property buyers, home emergency calls), and map out competitor gaps in your local market.

2

Information Strategy & Wireframing

We establish the structure (sitemap) of your pages and draft low-fidelity layout plans. This ensures every service section flows directly into a call-to-action.

3

Visual UI/UX Design

I craft elegant, premium mockups featuring clear hierarchy, curated typography, and on-brand colors. We align design styles with elite Silicon Valley standards.

4

Native Web Development

I convert layouts into lightweight, clean, fast code using semantic HTML5, custom CSS layout variables, and modular ES6 Javascript. No template bloat.

5

Rigorous QA & Optimization

We test every interactive element on Safari, Chrome, iOS, and Android. We audit loading speeds on Google PageSpeed to target 95+ performance metrics.

6

Deploy & Launch

We push your site live on highly optimized, secure hosting infrastructure. We connect domain configurations, setup SSL certificates, and verify lead forms.

7

Support & Maintenance

We stay connected. I offer ongoing server checks, regular database backups, uptime monitoring, and priority changes on demand.
