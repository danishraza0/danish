Architectural Case Studies: From Research to Results.

An in-depth breakdown of how three distinct concept projects were designed, coded, and measured to solve local service problems.

1. Client Overview

The Bistro is an established local Italian restaurant facing declining walk-in volumes. They wanted to capture diners searching Google Maps and allow table reservations online.

2. Business Problem

Their old website was slow, not mobile-friendly, and buried the menu inside a 12MB PDF. Potential customers left before finding table reservation links.

3. Project Goals

Shift 40%+ of reservation calls to online forms, make menus interactive, and optimize mobile load times to under 1 second.

4. User UX Research

Analyzed competing local eateries. Found that 75%+ of users evaluation is done on mobile phones during evening commutes, prioritizing immediate reservation buttons.

5. Visual Layout Strategy

Prioritize food photography, move reservation CTAs above the fold, and highlight Google reviews near the top banner to build immediate trust.

6. Wireframes & Flows

Designed a single-page funnel that guides visitors from header branding, through dinner menus, straight to a simplified booking form.

7. User Interface Design

Warm neutral background palette paired with elegant typography, ensuring the food remains the visual focus of the page.

8. Development Process

Custom-coded using HTML5 and pure CSS3, integrating lazy-loading scripts and lightweight SVGs for rapid mobile performance.

9. Estimated Business Results

A projected 45% increase in online reservations, a 30% reduction in phone administrative tasks, and a PageSpeed Insights performance score of 98/100.

10. Key Lessons Learned

Replacing bulky PDF menu assets with search engine crawlable HTML text drastically improved the restaurant's local SEO rankings.

1. Client Overview

Apex Legal is a regional business litigation law firm looking to upgrade their online presence and capture higher-value corporate consultations.

2. Business Problem

Their template site felt generic, did not display lawyer credentials, and failed to guide potential clients to consultation forms.

3. Project Goals

Redesign the site around practice area authority, showcase case results, and qualify leads through a structured contact form.

4. User UX Research

Evaluated how corporate buyers hire lawyers online. Learned that credentials, direct lawyer bios, and practice areas are the top trust factors.

5. Visual Layout Strategy

Use a clean, authoritative color scheme, high-contrast typography, and dedicated subpages for key practice areas.

6. Wireframes & Flows

Created a multi-page structure with clean navigation links, keeping the consultation request accessible from every footer.

7. User Interface Design

Minimal, elegant corporate layout utilizing dark slate tones and precise borders, conveying seriousness and professionalism.

8. Development Process

Built with accessible semantic markup and JSON-LD schema schemas to boost search engine visibility for business law queries.

9. Estimated Business Results

A projected 35% increase in high-quality business consultations, stronger local rankings, and complete PWA mobile readiness.

10. Key Lessons Learned

Structuring attorney profiles with clear credentials significantly increased form conversions from corporate visitors.

1. Client Overview

AirFlow is an air conditioning repair business losing emergency calls to faster-responding local competitors.

2. Business Problem

Their old website buried contact information, causing urgent mobile searchers to leave and click elsewhere.

3. Project Goals

Put a prominent "Call Now" button at the top of the mobile screen, add service area maps, and improve load speed under spotty connections.

4. User UX Research

Studied search behavior for emergency home services. Verified that speed-to-contact is the single biggest factor in winning the job.

5. Visual Layout Strategy

Bold, high-contrast colors and clean components sized for quick mobile interaction, since most emergency searches happen on a phone.

6. Wireframes & Flows

A persistent sticky mobile header with a click-to-call button and a simplified emergency service request form.

7. User Interface Design

High-contrast blue and orange tones, highlighting service areas, pricing, and license numbers prominently.

8. Development Process

Optimized image loading and deferred offscreen styling assets to target a Lighthouse Performance score of 99/100.

9. Estimated Business Results

A projected 60%+ increase in mobile organic dispatch calls, lower form abandonment, and a Lighthouse speed score of 99/100.

10. Key Lessons Learned

In emergency services, speed-to-load and one-click contact channels are more critical than long-form business histories.
